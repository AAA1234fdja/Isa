## Interpretation and limitations

The scores and completion counts are transcribed from the supplied report;
they have not been independently verified. The report includes truncated
response excerpts, but does not provide the scoring rubric, official benchmark
instance identifiers, runnable predictions, execution logs, or test results
needed to reproduce or independently validate the assessment. Response
completion is not the same as resolving an issue, and these figures must not be
interpreted as an official SWE-bench score or as proof that patches were applied
and passed tests. They describe this reported assessment, not the performance of
every ISA configuration or runtime.
